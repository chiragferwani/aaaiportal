How It Works

Everything you need to know about the Day 4 RAG Hackathon — from qualification to final results.

The Competition

Two rounds separate the best from the rest.
Round 1

Qualification Quiz

All participants take a quiz. The top-performing teams qualify for Round 2.

  • Open to all teams
  • Knowledge-based assessment
  • Top 25 teams advance
Round 2

RAG Build Challenge

25 qualified teams choose a theme and build a RAG chatbot in 4 hours.

  • 4 members per team
  • Choose 1 of 5 themes
  • 4-hour build window
  • Deploy & submit chatbot
